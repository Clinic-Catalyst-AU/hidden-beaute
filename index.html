The big idea

From "book with Rachael" to "book with Hidden Beauté"

You're full. Your team isn't. So the fastest growth isn't more demand for you. It's moving trust onto your team.

When a client sees an opening with another therapist, we want her to think "perfect, I know her, I've seen her videos," not "I'll wait for Rachael."

That's also what a future buyer pays for: a known brand, a strong team and systems that don't depend on the owner.

Content that does it

  • Meet your therapist, and what she loves doing most
  • "What I'd recommend for..." tips from each team member
  • Behind the scenes and training days
  • Client skin journeys with different therapists
  • Your humour. Everyone loves a silly reel, so keep them coming
Beauty salon to skin destination

Four clear pathways instead of a long menu

Clients don't search for machines. They search for what's bugging them. The consultation decides the treatment.

Pigmentation + sun damage

Your IPL hero category. Sun spots, uneven tone, overall rejuvenation.

Ageing well + collagen

Fine lines, texture, skin quality. Your Collagen Boosting Therapy ($290 / $330) already sits here.

Corrective skin

Congestion, scarring, dullness. Peels, needling and LED in the right order.

Beauty maintenance

Brows, lashes, waxing. Your regulars, and your cheapest path to new skin clients.

SEO + website quick wins

9 things you or Ali can do this week

We went through your current website (hiddenbeautestudio.com) page by page. Your goal is to show up for "Busselton skin" and "Busselton IPL". These get you moving.

1

Build a dedicated IPL page before launch

Nothing on the site mentions IPL yet. Google needs a page to rank. The IPL mockup is ready to copy, including the FAQ section Google and AI search love.

2

Give each skin treatment its own page

Right now collagen boosting and the herbal resurfacing facial share one "Advanced Skin Treatments" page. Split them out: IPL Busselton, Skin Needling Busselton, Skin Peels Busselton, LED Light Therapy Busselton, Skin Consultation Busselton.

3

Put "skin" in your skin page titles

Your skin pages are titled "Busselton Beauty Salon". Try "Advanced Skin Treatments Busselton | Hidden Beauté Studio" so Google connects you with skin searches.

4

Fix your main page headings

The moving banner (SKIN · MAKEUP · WAXING) is set as a main heading on most pages, so that's the first thing Google reads. Set the banner to normal text and give each page one clear heading, like "Skin treatments in Busselton".

5

Add page descriptions

Your About, Treatments and Contact pages have no description, so Google writes its own. In Squarespace: page settings, SEO, add a one-line description with "Busselton".

6

Install the Meta Pixel and Google tag

Do this before any ads run, so we can see who visits, who enquires and what each new client costs. Squarespace has a built-in spot for both under Marketing.

7

Connect your newsletter sign-up

Squarespace's code shows the sign-up box on your site has no storage location set. That can mean sign-ups aren't being saved anywhere. Worth a two-minute check in the block settings.

8

Add your business details to Google's language

The site has no "local business" markup (name, address, hours, services in a format search engines read). We've included it in the IPL mockup's code for Ali to copy.

9

Tidy the little things

Saturday hours on the contact page say "Make upn & Hair". Your sitemap also still lists old pages like /weddingsx and /contact-hidden-beaute-studio, and Collaborations lives at /team-2-2-1. Small things, but they all add up.

Your next acquisition campaign

The IPL launch, before 1 December

IPL arriving gives you the perfect reason to get in front of new people, before a new clinic opens on your street. But we wouldn't position Hidden Beauté as the cheap option. Local IPL pricing sits anywhere from $99 to $349, and you're the established, high-standard studio.

So instead of discounting, add value:

Launch offer (our suggestion)

Full face IPL $299, plus a complimentary neck + décolletage upgrade. Limited launch spots, consultation first.

Front door offer

$49 Advanced Skin Consultation + LED. The aim isn't the $49. It's a qualified skin client who leaves with a plan.

Suggested timing

Oct

Quick wins 1-9. Pixel on. IPL page live. Waitlist open. Email your beauty regulars (sequence 3).

Early Nov

IPL arrives. Film the team doing demos and "what it feels like" videos. Waitlist gets first access (sequence 2).

Nov

Meta ads: educational videos first, then the $49 consult and IPL offers to people who watched. Google ads on "IPL Busselton" searches.

Before 1 Dec

Hidden Beauté Skin Night (below), and your Google reviews system running hard.

Change the event model

Hidden Beauté Skin Night

120 people came to your launch. Four bought tickets to the birthday event. That tells us people love your events, and the $110 ticket was the barrier.

Next time, invite your VIPs free (a ticket "valued at $49") and ask them to bring a friend. Skin education, an IPL demo, skin analysis, mini LED, meet the team, and booking incentives on the night.

The goal isn't ticket money. It's new clients, referrals and treatment plans. Brief the team early so they're talking about it too.

The system behind it

So none of this relies on you remembering

Timely keeps doing what it does well: bookings. A CRM sits alongside it to run the marketing journey, so every enquiry gets followed up, every client gets rebooked, and you can see what's working.

Meta + Google + SEO + Social→Website + lead forms→CRM→Instant SMS + email→Consultation→Treatment plan→Rebook→Review + referral→Reactivation

Custom AI receptionist

Trained on your services, prices, hours, IPL, weddings and policies. It answers Instagram and Facebook enquiries day and night, sends your Timely link, and hands over to the team when it should.

Google reviews on autopilot

Every appointment triggers a friendly review request with your direct Google link. Reviews lift your Maps ranking, your website and your ads, and they're a moat a new clinic can't copy quickly.

Wedding automation

Your Diary of the Bride forms are a great start. A CRM can carry it from enquiry to deposit, timeline, reminders, review and anniversary offers.

Database reactivation

Beauty clients who've never tried skin, clients quiet for 3-6 or 12+ months, past needling and pigmentation clients. They already trust you, so they're cheaper than strangers.

A one-screen dashboard

Revenue, new clients by source, team utilisation, rebooking rate, consult-to-plan conversion and cost per new client. Decisions from data, not "it felt busy".

Content system

Educate 35% · Connect 30% · Prove 20% · Promote 15%. A simple way for the team to capture raw clips so you're not creating everything yourself.

About the new clinic

Don't race them to the bottom.

Keep an eye on their pricing, absolutely. But don't drop yours. Double down on what a newcomer can't copy: ten years of client relationships, your team's training, reviews, community, your database, and consistent follow-up.

And on renting a room for injectables: only if it fits the brand and the numbers. Don't add services out of fear.
